Help & User Manual

    
Tapehead · by Deon

    


  
Welcome to Tapehead

  
Your mobile studio for lyrics, beats, vocals, and sharing — so no idea has to disappear.


  
The story behind Tapehead

  
So many artists hum a melody on the walk home, write a line in Notes, or freestyle a hook — and by morning it’s gone. Ideas live for a moment, then get buried under everyday noise.

  
Tapehead was built to catch those moments: the hum, the first lines, the half-finished verse, the beat in your head — and turn them into a real session you can finish, mix, and share.

  
Founded by David Gideon Katimbo (a.k.a. Deon), Tapehead comes from a simple belief: creativity shouldn’t be lost to memory. If you can feel it, you should be able to save it, shape it, and send it out into the world.


  
What you can do

  
    
    
  • Write — lyrics by section (Intro, Verse, Chorus…)
    • 
    
  • Keys — hum a melody into chords, build progressions
    • 
    
  • Beat — step sequencer with genre kits
    • 
    
  • Record — sing over your beat and chords
    • 
    
  • Mix — balance tracks and bounce a full song
    • 
    
  • Feed — share work and discover other artists
    • 
    
  • Collab — rooms to co-write and layer ideas
    • 
  


  
Quick start (first 10 minutes)

  
    
    
  1. Create an account — email + artist name. New accounts get a 30-day Pro trial.
    2. 
    
  2. Open the menu (☰) → Write.
    3. 
    
  3. Set a title and type a prompt or first lines.
    4. 
    
  4. Use Finish Verse if you want AI help from your own words.
    5. 
    
  5. Go to Beat — pick a genre kit, tap steps, press Play.
    6. 
    
  6. Open Keys — try Hum → Chord or tap a progression.
    7. 
    
  7. Record — choose a section on the timeline, tap REC, sing.
    8. 
    
  8. Mix — balance levels → Bounce Full Song for a WAV.
    9. 
    
  9. Share to Feed so others can hear and collab.
    10. 
  


  
Write

  
Write is your lyric desk.

  
    
    
  • Use section tabs (Verse, Chorus…) so structure stays clear.
    • 
    
  • Tap ＋ Section to add a block.
    • 
    
  • Syllable count helps fit lines to the beat.
    • 
    
  • AI buttons need a prompt or first lines — they build on what you started, not empty air.
    • 
    
  • Share from Write opens the same publish sheet as Mix.
    • 
  


  
Keys

  
    
    
  • Hum → Chord — tap the mic, hum a short melody, get chord suggestions.
    • 
    
  • Build a progression in the four slots (I–V–vi–IV style).
    • 
    
  • Play the piano pads; set octave as needed.
    • 
    
  • Your BPM from the project is the same clock as Beat and Record.
    • 
  


  
Beat

  
    
    
  • Choose 8 / 16 / 32 steps (16 = two bars by default).
    • 
    
  • Tap pads to place Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Clap.
    • 
    
  • Randomize or Duplicate for quick ideas; Clear asks for confirm.
    • 
    
  • Genre kit changes the drum character.
    • 
  


  
Record

  
    
    
  • Pick a section on the timeline (bars are mapped: e.g. Chorus later than Verse 1).
    • 
    
  • You’ll hear beat + chords + click while recording.
    • 
    
  • Tap the red REC, sing, stop when done.
    • 
    
  • Use Nudge if the take feels early or late.
    • 
    
  • Send to Mix to balance the vocal against the beat.
    • 
  


  
Mix & Bounce

  
    
    
  • Faders for Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Clap, Chords, Vocal — plus Mute / Solo.
    • 
    
  • Checklist reminds you: title, verse+chorus, beat, healthy levels.
    • 
    
  • Bounce Full Song renders drums + chords + vocal to a WAV file on your device.
    • 
    
  • Publish Session posts selected parts (lyrics, beat, keys, mix, vocal) to the Feed.
    • 
  


  
Feed

  
    
    
  • ＋ Post to share your current session.
    • 
    
  • Tap ▶ on a card to hear beat / vocal / full song when available.
    • 
    
  • Open loads that post into your projects so you can learn or remix.
    • 
    
  • Like, comment, and filter by Latest, Popular, My posts, Open to collab, genre, or Has sound.
    • 
  


  
Collab

  
    
    
  • Create Room from your current project and share the code.
    • 
    
  • Join with a code (Paste works when the browser allows).
    • 
    
  • Use rooms to split verses, share a beat, and build together.
    • 
  


  
Profile & Pro

  
    
    
  • Avatar or menu user block → Profile (bio, genres, links, open to collab).
    • 
    
  • Pro unlocks AI depth, collab rooms, and unlimited projects after the free tier limits.
    • 
    
  • Trial starts with your Cloud account; upgrades are verified server-side after payment confirmation.
    • 
  


  
Tips for beginners

  
    
    
  • Start with one verse + chorus and a simple 16-step beat.
    • 
    
  • Record in a quiet place; headphones help you hear the bed.
    • 
    
  • Name projects clearly (or keep the auto date · genre · BPM names).
    • 
    
  • Save often — projects store on your device (and cloud when configured).
    • 
    
  • Share early drafts on Feed as “open to collab” to find partners.
    •
