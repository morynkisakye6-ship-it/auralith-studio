Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version. Auralith is a music studio. We collect what we need to run your account, save and share your work, power the AI tools and process payments. We do not sell your personal data, we show no ads, and we use no advertising or analytics trackers. You can delete your account inside the app.

1. Who we are

Auralith Pro (“Auralith”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile music studio for writing lyrics, making beats, recording vocals, mixing and collaborating. It is operated by David Gideon Katimbo (“Deon”). Questions about this policy: burstcomepsp@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text), artist nameTo create and secure your account and let you sign in.
Profile details you add: photo, bio, location, genres, links, “open to collaborate”To build your creator profile. Parts of your profile are visible to other users.
Your creative work: lyrics, beats, chord progressions, mix settings, project names, and vocal recordings you save to the cloud, publish or use in collab roomsTo save, sync, share and collaborate on your work.
Community activity: Feed posts, comments, likes, follows, direct messages, collab room messages, blocks and reportsTo provide the social features and keep the community safe.
AI requests: the text you submit to AI tools (prompts, lyrics, section context, style options)To generate the lyrics and suggestions you ask for.
Payment records: plan, amount, currency, transaction reference and statusTo activate your Pro plan and prevent fraud. We do not receive or store your card number or mobile-money PIN.
Technical data: IP address and request detailsShort-lived use for security and rate limiting, to stop abuse.

Microphone. Auralith asks for microphone access only when you record a vocal or hum a melody. Recordings stay on your device unless you choose to save them to the cloud, publish them or use them in a collab room.

On your device. The app stores settings, your sign-in session and, in guest mode, your projects in your device’s local storage. Guest projects are not uploaded.

3. How we use it

  • To provide, maintain and improve Auralith.
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • To process Pro payments and start your free trial.
  • To show your public content and profile to other users as you choose.
  • To respond to support requests and reports, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your content to train AI models ourselves, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

We use these service providers to run Auralith. They process data on our behalf:

Other people can see what you choose to make public (profile, Feed posts, comments), what you share in collab rooms with members, and messages you send them. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users and Auralith.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and cloud content: until you delete them or your account.
  • AI job records: automatically expire after 24 hours.
  • Payment records: kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and fraud prevention.
  • Backups and logs: removed on a rolling basis.

6. Deleting your account

Open Profile → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and the private audio files you uploaded. This cannot be undone. If you cannot use the app, email us at burstcomepsp@gmail.com from your account address. Anything you published that others have already copied or downloaded may remain on their devices.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. You can edit your profile and delete your content in the app. For anything else, email burstcomepsp@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. We rely on our providers’ safeguards for these transfers.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls on private files and per-user permissions on stored data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Auralith is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account (or older where your country requires parental consent). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Continuing to use Auralith after a change means you accept the updated policy.
